1-on-1 Tutoring

Prefer to learn 1-on-1 instead of in a group?

Same qualified teachers, a schedule built around you, and lessons shaped entirely by your goals — whether that's exam prep, catching up before rejoining a group, or steady conversation practice. Fill in the form below and we'll match you with a teacher.

How this is different from a group class?

1-on-1 tutoring provides dedicated, individualised instruction tailored specifically to your needs, goals, and learning pace. Unlike a group class, where the lesson structure and teaching time are shared among several participants, a 1-on-1 session is focused entirely on one learner and is priced separately from our standard group-class tuition.

The information provided in this form allows us to understand your needs and prepare the most suitable tutoring arrangement. — nothing is billed by submitting it.

Tutoring Rates

Our 1-on-1 tutoring offers focused, personalized support tailored to your goals, language level, and individual learning pace. Because every session is customized to your needs, 1-on-1 tutoring is priced separately from our group course tuition.
