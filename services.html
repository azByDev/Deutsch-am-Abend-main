Courses & Prices

Find your level. Get started.

All courses follow CEFR standards (A1–B2) and are taught in small groups. Regular and discounted prices are shown below — see who qualifies for the discount further down this page.

German Language Courses

A1 to B2 — choose your starting point

Level A1

Beginner

No prior knowledge needed. Learn the alphabet, greetings, numbers, and confident basic daily conversation.

Prerequisite: None — absolute beginners
Core Skills: Alphabet, greetings, shopping & daily basics
Curriculum: 165 units · 120+ instruction hours
Class Format: Small groups · Interactive speaking from Day 1
Target: Goethe-Zertifikat A1 exam alignment
A1 · View Schedule
Level A2

Elementary

Build on A1: daily routines, shopping, travel, practical grammar, and simple past-tense conversations.

Prerequisite: Completed A1 or basic elementary German
Core Skills: Past tense (Perfekt), travel & work routines
Curriculum: 165 units · 120+ instruction hours
Practice Focus: Real-life conversation & fluid dialogue
Target: Goethe-Zertifikat A2 exam alignment
A2 · View Schedule
Level B1

Intermediate

Gain independence: express opinions, handle work and daily situations, and understand longer dialogues.

Prerequisite: Completed A2 or equivalent level
Core Skills: Express opinions, workplace & residency German
Curriculum: 165 units · 120+ instruction hours
Key Pathways: Visa, permanent residency & Ausbildung prep
Target: Goethe-Zertifikat B1 (Visa & Ausbildung)
B1 · View Schedule
Level B2

Upper Intermediate

Reach professional fluency: discuss complex topics, write clearly, and prepare for work or study abroad.

Prerequisite: Completed B1 or equivalent level
Core Skills: Technical discussion, debate & nuanced writing
Curriculum: 256 units · 190+ instruction hours
Key Pathways: German university & clinical license recognition
Target: Goethe-Zertifikat B2 (University & Nursing)
B2 · View Schedule

Flexible Learning Tracks

Choose the schedule that fits your life

All tracks cover the identical CEFR-aligned curriculum and full 120+ instruction hours. Choose your weekly frequency based on how fast you want to progress.

⚡ Fastest Track

Intensive Track

5x a week · Mon to Fri

3 hours daily · 15 hours / week

≈ 8–9 weeks to finish 1 full level (120+ hrs)
  • Best for: Visa applicants, Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte), Ausbildung, or upcoming job start dates.
  • Batches: Morning (08:00–11:00) & Evening (18:00–21:00)
📅 Most Popular

Semi-Intensive

3x a week · Mon / Tue / Wed

3 hours per session · 9 hours / week

≈ 13–14 weeks to finish 1 full level (120+ hrs)
  • Best for: University students and working learners who want steady progress without daily class pressure.
  • Batches: Evening (18:00–21:00) · Morning batches on request

Discounted Pricing

Who can get the discounted price?

SPFL-PASCH Program

You qualify for the discounted course fee automatically if you are:

  • an alumnus/alumna of the SPFL-PASCH program, or
  • a current SPFL-PASCH German student

Not in SPFL-PASCH?

You can still receive the discounted registration fee by becoming a Deutsch am Abend Community Member — see details below.

Community Membership · Valid for 2 years

Become a Community Member!

If you're not eligible for the SPFL-PASCH discount, you can unlock the same discounted course pricing by joining as a Deutsch am Abend Community Member.

  • Discounted registration fees on every course
  • Access to intercultural community events
  • Online Study Lounge / Tutorial sessions (1–2x per week)
  • Membership is valid for 2 years from signing
How to apply:
  1. Download the membership application form (PDF) below.
  2. Fill it in and sign it.
  3. Pay the membership fee — ₱500 (students) or ₱1,000 (non-students) — as instructed on the form.
  4. Email the signed form together with your proof of payment to info@deutschamabend.org.
  5. We'll confirm your membership and apply your discounted course pricing within 2–3 business days.
Download Membership Form (PDF)

Remarks: If you are not eligible for the SPFL-PASCH discount, purchasing Community Membership is usually worthwhile because the registration discount is greater than the membership cost.
